The naive join repeats each payment for every item. The safe total sums payment records at their original grain. These diagnostic sums omit unparseable amounts; use the invalid_payment check before treating them as complete totals. Neither total represents recognized revenue or profit.

Evidence and policy

Every rule has a CSV in exceptions/ and executable SQL in rules.json. Failed-record counts are per rule and must not be added as unique defective records. Errors block --strict; warnings remain investigation items. Payment reconciliation uses a BRL 0.05 tolerance for rounding. Rules are trusted local configuration, not untrusted user input.
